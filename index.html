Why we exist

Ambition was never the barrier. Infrastructure was.

Building a business takes capital, networks, and mentors — and those are distributed unevenly. Immigrant high school students bear the consequences. We built the infrastructure they were missing.

01

No access to capital

56% of immigrant teens cite personal finances as a top barrier. Without seed funding or guidance, great ideas never move beyond a thought.

02

No visible role models

Immigrant students rarely meet founders who share their background. Without them, entrepreneurship feels like something other people do.

03

No structured learning

Existing programs target students who already have networks. None address the cultural and financial context immigrant students navigate.

“Immigrants are a driving force behind America’s prosperity… these companies generated $8.6 trillion in revenue.”

The talent is here. Root to Ventures makes the path visible — and reachable — for the next generation.

The program

From a first idea to a real investor — in two phases.

Four Saturdays of hands-on building, then a live Demo Day with a venture capitalist. Every student leaves with a pitch, a plan, and lifetime access to Venture.bot.

Explore the full curriculum
Phase 1

The Bootcamp

Four Saturdays of ideation, finance, marketing, and pitching. Every day opens with a guest lecture from an immigrant entrepreneur, then an interactive activity, then Venture.bot-supported build time.

Phase 2

VC Demo Day

Ten immigrant student founders pitch to a Sierra Ventures partner who sold his company for $510M — with live Q&A and real investor feedback.

Real seed funding

The top three pitches receive real capital — $1,000, $100 and $75 in Cohort 1 — plus a guaranteed spot at Demo Day.

An AI co-founder for life

Venture.bot, trained on every mentor session, stays with students long after the bootcamp ends — 16 modules, a chat co-founder, and a VC simulator.

100% free. Always.

Funded by local sponsors and a community of immigrant entrepreneurs paying it forward. No fees, no prerequisites, no gatekeeping.

Four Saturdays

Each day builds on the last.

01
Day 1 · Problem & Solution

Find a problem worth solving

“Problem Roulette,” the Problem → Solution → Value framework, and B2C / B2B / B2B2C models. Students leave with a problem statement, a solution, and a business model.

02
Day 2 · Finance & Marketing

Make the numbers work

“Guess That Price,” the 4 Ps, STP, fixed vs. variable costs, burn rate and runway. Students build a full go-to-market and financial plan with Venture.bot.

03
Day 3 · Pitch Development

Tell the story

“Pitch in a Minute,” the Hook → Story → Ask method, and a slide-by-slide deck template. Students transform raw templates into compelling pitch decks.

04
Day 4 · Final Pitch Competition

Take the stage

Five finalists pitch in Liberty High School’s auditorium to 100+ family, friends and judges. The top three win seed funding and a seat at VC Demo Day.
