Lessons learned

What we’d do differently.

Daily Plan–Do–Check–Act cycles kept the cohort improving week to week. These are the changes that carry into Cohort 2.

01

Start outreach four weeks earlier

Mentor and sponsor commitments landed close to launch and forced last-minute adjustments. Hard deadlines, set earlier, keep the timeline stable.

02

Send a 24-hour checklist

Students often arrived unclear on logistics, eating into instruction time. A pre-session checklist protects every minute of the two hours.

03

Track attendance from Day 1

Inconsistent early tracking made drop-off patterns hard to spot. Formal attendance makes every PDCA cycle evidence-based.

04

Multi-channel recruitment works

A recruitment push across channels plus a nonprofit partner with 29 DFW chapters brought 113 registrants — clearing the target before launch.

05

Nightly Venture.bot updates work

Updating the platform the same evening with each mentor’s frameworks kept it current — and kept students engaged between Saturdays.

06

Watch both tracks at once

Running Agile and Waterfall in parallel stretched attention thin; the Day 1 API rate-limit spike surfaced later than it should have. Dedicated monitoring per track fixes that.
