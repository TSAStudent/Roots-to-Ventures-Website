What’s next

We asked students what we should do next.

The post-survey asked one open question. We sorted the answers into themes, and these three are where we see the most room to grow.

01

Grow to more DFW schools

Train student leaders at other high schools to run their own Roots to Ventures chapter, all feeding into one big Pitch Day.

02

Bring some workshops in person

Keep the online workshops for reach, but add one in-person session at Nancy Watten Center so teams can build face to face before Pitch Day.

03

Turn the VC meeting into a pipeline

Add an investor-ready track in Venture.bot and open the VC meeting to more teams, so good ideas have a real path to funding.
