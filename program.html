The Bootcamp · Phase 1 & Phase 2

Four Saturdays. One Demo Day. A venture of your own.

Root to Ventures compresses the first six months of a startup into one structured month — problem, numbers, pitch, competition — with a real immigrant entrepreneur in the room every single week.

Two phases

From a blank page to a VC pitch.

The bootcamp runs as a fixed Waterfall sequence — each Saturday depends on the one before. Venture.bot runs alongside it in Agile sprints, updated the same night with what the mentor taught.

Phase 1 · November

The four-day bootcamp

Problem & solution, finance & marketing, pitch development, and a live pitch competition. Each day pairs a guest lecture from an immigrant entrepreneur with hands-on activities and work time with Venture.bot.

Phase 2 · December

VC Demo Day

Ten teams — our top three plus seven immigrant student founders scouted nationally — pitch for ten minutes each to Shashank Saxena of Sierra Ventures, followed by live Q&A.

Anatomy of a Saturday

Two hours, zero lectures you’ll forget.

Every session follows the same rhythm, so students always know what comes next — and every minute of it is designed around doing, not listening.

3:00
5 min

Pre-survey

Students rate their knowledge, confidence and interest. On Day 1 this is the full pre-program assessment.

3:05
20 min

Guest lecture

An immigrant entrepreneur tells their story and teaches the day’s core framework — from a VC partner to a banker to an independent investor.

3:25
10 min

Interactive activity

“Problem Roulette,” “Guess That Price,” and other games that make the framework stick before anyone opens a laptop.

3:35
40 min

Team presentation

Our team walks through the frameworks step by step — business models, the 4 Ps, burn rate and runway, pitch deck anatomy.

4:15
40 min

Work time + Venture.bot

Teams apply the framework to their own venture, with mentors circulating and Venture.bot answering the questions in between.

4:55
5 min

Post-survey & wrap

The same questions, answered again — so growth is measured every single week, not assumed.

The curriculum

Four days, four Saturdays.

01
Day 1 · Problem & Solution · Mentor: Prithviraj Logan

Find a problem worth solving

Students play “Problem Roulette,” then learn the Problem → Solution → Value framework and the difference between B2C, B2B and B2B2C. Teams form around a shared problem and leave with a one-line business model. That night, Venture.bot is updated with the ideation frameworks taught in the room.

Problem statementBusiness modelTeam formed
02
Day 2 · Finance & Marketing · Mentor: Ross Gillispie

Make the numbers work

“Guess That Price” kicks off a session on customer personas, the 4 Ps, segmentation-targeting-positioning, fixed vs. variable costs, pricing strategy, and burn rate and runway. Every team builds a go-to-market plan and a simple financial model with Venture.bot.

Customer personaGTM planFinancial model
03
Day 3 · Pitch Development · Mentor: Srinivas Maram

Tell the story investors remember

Pitch deck anatomy, Hook → Story → Ask, and design principles that turn a template into a deck people remember. Teams submit their full deck for review; mentors and our team select the top five to advance. The day closes with the post-program assessment.

Full pitch deckDeck submittedTop 5 selected
04
Day 4 · Pitch Competition · Judges: Maram, Logan, Arun

Pitch live. Win seed funding.

Five finalists take the stage in the Liberty High School auditorium in front of 100+ family members, friends and judges. The top three receive seed funding — $1,000, $100 and $75 — plus a guaranteed slot at the VC Demo Day with Sierra Ventures.

$1,175 awardedLive Q&ADemo Day slot
Who it’s for

No experience required. Ambition is.

Root to Ventures is built for students who have the drive to build something but not the capital, network or role models to take a first step.

01

Immigrant or first-generation

You or your parents immigrated to the U.S. Every mentor shares that experience — so the advice is about your reality, not a generic one.

02

In high school in DFW

Grades 9–12 across Dallas–Fort Worth. Sessions are in Plano and Frisco on Saturday afternoons; the Demo Day is on Zoom.

03

Willing to show up four times

Each Saturday builds on the last. Come to all four, do the work between sessions with Venture.bot, and you’ll leave with a real venture.
