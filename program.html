A free four-week program

Three workshops. One Pitch Day. A venture of your own.

Roots to Ventures takes first-gen immigrant high school students from “I have an idea” to pitching it in front of judges in four Saturdays. Online so no one needs a ride, on Saturday mornings so it does not clash with school or work.

The schedule

Each Saturday builds on the last.

01
Oct 3 · Zoom · Problem & Solution

Find a problem worth solving

Led by Rithvik Gabri, Stanford student and cofounder of Y Combinator-backed Bizzy AI. Students learn to spot a real problem, define who has it, and shape a solution people would actually use. Teams form and get their first Venture.bot task.

02
Oct 10 · Zoom · Finance & Marketing

Make the numbers and the message work

Build a simple business model, estimate costs and pricing, and plan how to reach your first customers. Students leave with a financial plan and a marketing strategy they can defend.

03
Oct 17 · Zoom · Pitch Development

Tell the story

Led by Sri Vasireddy, ex-CTO of Hitachi Vantara and founder of REAN Cloud. Students turn their plan into a pitch deck, practice delivery, and submit their decks before Pitch Day.

04
Oct 24 · In person · Nancy Watten Center High School

Pitch Day

Teams pitch live to a panel of judges in front of families and friends. The top three win seed prizes and a seat at a private meeting with venture capitalist Anaiy Somalwar.

A typical Saturday

Two hours, zero lectures you half-watch.

Every workshop mixes a mentor talk with games, small breakout rooms and hands-on team time, so cameras stay on and ideas actually move.

  1. 10:00 · Check-in poll

    A quick poll to see where every team stands since last week.

  2. 10:10 · Mentor talk

    A founder or professor teaches the week’s framework from their own experience.

  3. 10:40 · Breakout game

    Rooms of 3–4 students play a game that puts the framework to work.

  4. 11:00 · Our lesson

    The Roots to Ventures team walks through the week’s deliverable step by step.

  5. 11:30 · Team build + Venture.bot

    Teams build their deliverable with Venture.bot answering questions live.

  6. 11:55 · Exit survey

    Students tell us what landed and what did not, and we adjust the next workshop.

Who it’s for

No experience required. Just an idea.

We built the program around first-gen students’ real lives.

  • High school students in Dallas–Fort Worth from first-generation immigrant families.
  • Any idea, any stage. Most students arrive with nothing more than a notebook and a hunch.
  • Zero cost. Workshops, mentors, Venture.bot and Pitch Day are all free.
  • Online first. Workshops run on Zoom; only Pitch Day is in person.
  • Teams of 2–4. Come with friends or get matched in Workshop 1.
