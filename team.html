The people

Built by students. Backed by people who have done it.

As students from immigrant families, we grew up hearing about the sacrifices our parents made. We also saw how starting something of your own could feel out of reach without money, connections or guidance. So we built the program we wished we had.

Founding team

Three project managers. One mission.

Juniors at Ranchview High School in Irving, Texas. We split the work across venue & logistics, finance & outreach, and technology.

Project Manager · Venue & Logistics

Tarun Shanmugam

Booked Nancy Watten Center for Pitch Day and set up the room, ran the Zoom side of all three workshops, and kept mentors and judges on schedule with confirmations before every session.

Project Manager · Finance & Outreach

Sameer Ladha

Recruited 39 first-gen students through school clubs, counselors and Instagram, led sponsor outreach, tracked every dollar of the budget, set up the VC meeting, and led Workshop 2.

Project Manager · Technology

Surya Raineedi

Built the Roots to Ventures website and Venture.bot from scratch, updated it after every workshop with that mentor’s frameworks, led Workshop 3, and ran the pre/post survey analysis.

Mentors, judges & investor

People who have built something.

We picked mentors on three things: they had built something real, they could explain it simply, and they connected with first-gen students.

Workshop 1 mentor · Judge

Rithvik Gabri

Stanford · Cofounder, Bizzy AI

Cofounder of Bizzy AI, an AI agent that negotiates bills and refunds for customers, funded by Y Combinator. Led Problem & Solution.

Workshop 2 mentor · Head judge

Arun Kumar Ponnuswamy

CEO, CAP Digisoft · CEO, Jugl

Cloud communications founder who led Finance & Marketing and headed the Pitch Day judging panel.

Workshop 3 mentor

Sri Vasireddy

Founder, REAN Cloud · CEO, REAN Foundation

Former CTO of Hitachi Vantara. Led Pitch Development and coached teams on telling their story.

VC meeting · Venture capitalist

Anaiy Somalwar

Early-stage investor · ex Pear VC, Foundation Capital

Berkeley M.E.T. graduate who sourced $50M+ in startup deals. Met the top three teams, pushed on their numbers and gave honest investor feedback.

Plus two professors and two Nancy Watten Center teachers

Our professors reviewed the workshop plans and taught alongside the mentors; the teachers joined the Pitch Day judging panel, giving students five experienced voices in total.
