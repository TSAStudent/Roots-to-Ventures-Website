The people

Built by students. Backed by people who walked the road.

Root to Ventures was founded and run by three high school students in Frisco, Texas — and taught by five industry professionals who each carry a personal connection to the immigrant experience.

Founding team

Three project managers. One mission.

We split the work across venue & logistics, finance & outreach, and technology — and ran the bootcamp as Waterfall and Venture.bot as Agile, side by side.

Project Manager · Venue & Logistics

Ish Aggarwal

Secured the primary and contingency venues, reviewed every Venture.bot module before launch, and coordinated mentor logistics ahead of each bootcamp day.

Project Manager · Finance & Outreach

Rayanne Noonari

Led sponsorship outreach and mentor and student recruitment — raising $2,320 — and scouted seven immigrant student founders nationally for the VC Demo Day.

Project Manager · Technology & Venture.bot

Nithilan Karthik

Wrote the Venture.bot RFC, built the 16-module platform and AI integration in Agile sprints, and shipped a post-day update after every Saturday.

Mentors & judges

Five professionals. Zero generic advice.

Bankers, investors, consultants and a venture capitalist who built and sold a $510M company — every one of them shaped the curriculum and Venture.bot with their own frameworks.

VC Mentor · Sierra Ventures

Shashank Saxena

Partner at Sierra Ventures. Built VNDLY from scratch and sold it to Workday for $510M. Hosted the VC Demo Day and now mentors our first-place founder.

Day 2 Mentor · American Bank of Commerce

Ross Gillispie

13+ years in banking. Taught financial planning, pricing strategy and cost structures — and pushed Venture.bot past generic advice.

Day 3 Mentor & Judge · Independent Investor

Srinivas Maram

Technology entrepreneur and real estate investor. Led pitch development with his Hook → Story → Ask method and judged the final.

Day 1 Mentor & Judge

Prithviraj Logan

Opened the bootcamp with problem identification and business model design, then returned to judge the pitch competition.

Judge · Pitch Competition

Parasu Arun

Judged the five finalists on Day 4 alongside Maram and Logan, scoring problem clarity, market, model and delivery.
