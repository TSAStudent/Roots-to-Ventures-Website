How it works

Built around the workshops, not instead of them.

Before writing a line of code, we wrote a short plan for what the website and AI would do and why, and our mentors marked it up. Two rules came out of it.

Advice for first-gen students

Venture.bot had to give advice that fits first-gen students’ lives, from money pressure to family expectations, not generic startup tips.

Follows the workshop order

Problem, then finance and marketing, then pitch. It reinforces the lesson students just had instead of running its own curriculum.

Smarter every week

After each workshop we add that mentor’s frameworks. Every update is tested by a teammate before it reaches students.

VC prep

Before the winners met a venture capitalist, Venture.bot drilled them on market size, costs and the questions investors ask.

Catches students slipping

It flags any student who has not logged in for a few days so we can reach out before they fall behind.

Open after the program

Venture.bot stays free and open to any student who wants to keep building, which is how it reached 312 users.
