Student application

Free. Four Saturdays. Real seed funding.

No business idea required — we’ll help you find one on Day 1. You just need to be an immigrant or first-generation high school student in DFW who can make it to four Saturday afternoons.

  1. Apply in five minutes

    Tell us a little about you. We read every application; there’s no essay.

  2. Get your welcome kit

    Logistics, a 24-hour pre-session checklist, and early access to Venture.bot.

  3. Show up on Day 1

    Find a problem worth solving, form a team, and start building — with a mentor in the room.

  4. Pitch for seed funding

    Top five pitch live; top three win funding and a slot at the VC Demo Day.

Mentor a Saturday

Teach the thing nobody taught you.

Our mentors are immigrant and first-generation professionals — bankers, investors, operators, founders. You give one Saturday afternoon: a 20-minute story and framework, then an hour alongside teams as they build.

  • Your framework becomes the curriculum. Venture.bot is updated that night with what you taught.
  • One afternoon, 3:00–5:00 PM. Judges return for the Day 4 pitch competition if they’d like.
  • We handle logistics. Venue, slides support, student questions, and a thank-you gift basket.
  • Real outcome. Cohort 1 mentors produced +52% knowledge and +111% interest growth.
